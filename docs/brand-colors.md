The October posts use: green #31662F (accents, ribbons), cream #F3F1EA (light backgrounds), brown #4C3634 (footer stripes),
sage grey #B1BCB1 (small labels on dark, thin rules), gold #D39C48 (donate and warning only).
Dark backgrounds use #1E3F1D, a darker shade of the brand green (the palette has no dark green). Body text #333130.
The older values #22371F, #156826, #DBE6A7, #59A66C and #C9A227 came from the repo brand guide and are not in this palette.
