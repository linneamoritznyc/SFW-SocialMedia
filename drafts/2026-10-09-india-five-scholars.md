# Five scholarships to the India workshop

## Why this is the best India post

Five named people travelling to a two-week workshop, with microscopes, who could
not otherwise have gone. The notes record Evan's framing directly: "putting the
offering in front of people closest to the land rather than only Western, US and
Eurocentric audiences who can afford thousands of dollars."

That sentence is the post. It just needs five faces attached to it.

## The five slides

1. **Five scholarships. One workshop. A set of microscopes.**
2 to 4. **One recipient each**, or two per slide if only three are reachable in
   time. Photograph, name, where they farm or work, one line in their words.
5. **What happens next.** They come out of the fortnight having run the practical
   work, and the microscopes stay with them.

## After the workshop

**These five become a Field Notes cohort.** That is the reason to run this post
now rather than after. If the audience meets them in October, their trial results
next year land on people who already know who they are.

Set that expectation inside the organisation now, not in March: somebody needs to
stay in touch with five people across a time difference, and that is a named job.
`sop/field-notes-series.md` covers the format; the supply line is the part that
needs an owner.

## What is missing

- **All five names.** Not in the notes.
- **All five photographs, and consent.** Same terms as the scholarship carousel:
  written yes, a handle, a way to decline that costs nothing. See
  `variants/scholarships/captions.md` for the email.
- **Whether they want to be named at all.** A scholarship is a financial fact
  about a person. Some will be glad to be named and some will not, and asking is
  not optional.
- **Who is asking them.** Sammie, Loide, Gerald and Kavi carried the work behind
  the tour, per the notes. One of them has the relationship. It should not be a
  cold email from social media.
