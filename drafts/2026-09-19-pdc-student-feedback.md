# Sixteen countries, one course

## Why this is worth posting

It is a testimonial post, which usually earns a like and nothing else. What makes
this one different is the spread: sixteen countries learning a discipline that is
site-specific by definition. Several students named that spread as the thing they
valued, unprompted, which turns a wall of praise into an actual observation.

The quotes are real, named and unedited. That clears the expertise bar on people
and places rather than on mechanism.

## Three things to settle before it runs

**1. Consent, and it is the gate.** These were written as course feedback and
shared internally by Delvin at Evan's invitation. That is not the same as
permission to put someone's name and words on a public grid. Every student quoted
says yes in writing, or their card comes out. Delvin holds the relationship and
should be the one to ask.

**2. The country list and the quotes disagree.** Delvin lists sixteen countries.
The quotes include students from the USA, Wales and Jersey, none of which are on
that list. So the list is partial, or the number is wrong, or both. **Do not
publish "sixteen" until somebody reconciles it against the enrolment record.** If
the real figure is higher, that is a better post anyway.

**3. One quote is left out on purpose.** Daniel Burnett's ends "excited to get
certified". "Certified" is on the banned list and trimming somebody's sentence to
dodge a word is worse than not using it. Alec Blenche's is also out: no country
given, so it cannot be attributed the way the others are.

## Notes on the copy

**"None of them will design the same garden"** is the only line in the caption
that is mine rather than theirs, and it is doing the work of the whole post.
Permaculture design responds to a specific site, so the same syllabus produces
sixteen different answers. That is the interesting fact hiding inside a
testimonial round-up.

**No enrolment link.** Cohort 2 started on 20 September, so a join CTA would be
pointing at a closed door, and a comment CTA is the right ask for a Community
Voice post anyway. If a link is wanted, it needs a verified URL: the September
posts for Mary Reynolds were sent to the registration page by mistake and nobody
caught it.

**The programme is written out in full.** Permaculture Design Certificate, never
the initials, per the context pack.
