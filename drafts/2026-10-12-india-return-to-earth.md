# Return to Earth

## Blocked, deliberately

The meeting notes carry one line: *"First tour, teaching alongside Vandana Shiva's
Return to Earth program."* That is the entire record.

**"Alongside" covers a very wide range.** It could mean a shared platform at the
same event. It could mean the two programmes run in the same window in the same
country. It could mean a Foundation session inside a Return to Earth programme,
or the reverse. Each of those is a different post and three of them are wrong.

The separate risk, already flagged on the earlier India work: **Vandana Shiva
appears in a Foundation webinar series with Evan Buckman**, which is real and is
recorded in `content/webinars.json`. She is also listed in the notes among
"authorities" alongside David Holmgren, Mary Cole and Adam Cobb, who "currently
exist only as webinar thumbnails". None of that is a booking for the India tour.

So: **nothing gets drafted here until somebody who was in the room says what was
agreed.** Ask Evan, and ask in a form that cannot be answered vaguely:

1. Is the Foundation appearing at a Return to Earth event, or are the two
   programmes running in parallel?
2. Is Vandana Shiva personally present at anything we are teaching at?
3. Has anyone from Return to Earth agreed to be named in our marketing?
4. Do they want a collaborative post, and who is the contact?

## What I would write once those are answered

**If it is a genuine shared platform,** it is one of the strongest posts of the
quarter and it should lead with the substance rather than the name: what the two
programmes disagree about is more interesting than what they share, and an
audience that follows both will know it.

**If it is parallel rather than shared,** the honest post is smaller and still
worth running: two programmes working on the same problem in the same country in
the same month, described accurately, with no implication of a joint platform.

**If it is neither,** there is no post, and the tour has three other events.

## The line that must not appear

Any phrasing that lets a reader conclude Vandana Shiva endorses the Foundation,
teaches our curriculum, or appears at our workshop, unless every one of those is
true and agreed in writing. She has an audience in the millions and a long
record of objecting publicly to having her name attached to things.
