# The community post

## The blocker, and it is yours to answer

**There is nowhere to send people.** The notes are explicit: the community has no
public front door on the site. A post inviting 4,680 people's worth of new members
to join something with no landing page is an invitation to a dead end.

**So this post needs a URL before it needs anything else, and I need you to tell
me where it lives.** Three options and I think the second one is right:

1. **A page on the new site**, `/community/join`. Correct long term. Blocked on
   the 1 October launch, which makes this a November or December post.
2. **The existing community platform's own join link**, in the bio, now. Ugly,
   works today, and the notes already treat the community as warm demand nobody
   is asking for.
3. **Wait for the site.** Safe, and it leaves the warmest audience the Foundation
   has untouched for another quarter.

The notes list "open a door for the 4,680 community members" as its own priority,
with a "public community page with a clear route into courses". **That page is
the answer. This post should be timed to it.** If the page slips, run option 2.

## What the number actually says, and how not to misuse it

207 contributors out of 4,680 is 4.4%. The notes read that correctly: for a free
community that is close to normal, and it means the other 95% are watching.

**So do not post this as "join our active community".** Post it as an invitation
to a room where most people are listening, because that is true and it is also
less intimidating than the alternative framing. The number of people who will
never post is the reason a quiet person might join.
