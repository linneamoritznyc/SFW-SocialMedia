# The scholarship numbers

The carousel is already built at `variants/scholarships/`. This draft carries the
figures the meeting notes add, and the two questions that block them.

## Two unresolved figures, both flagged in the notes themselves

**1. What period does the $250,000 cover?** The notes list this twice as an open
question. Allison asked it live in the meeting chat, independently, within
minutes. Section 7 asks: "Was the $250,000 in partial scholarships awarded across
full-year 2026 or since March?" **Ask Stephanie or Sofia.**

**2. Was June typical?** Section T5 asks whether June was a one-off around Dr.
Elaine's birthday or a permanent rate. The notes say June was "around Dr.
Elaine's birthday", which strongly suggests it was a campaign month rather than a
monthly rate. **If it was a one-off, say so.** It is more interesting, and it
makes the date meaningful instead of arbitrary.

Neither figure goes on a card until both are answered.

## What the notes add that the earlier draft did not have

- **June was Dr. Elaine's birthday month, and there was a Compost With Us
  campaign.** That is why the number is what it is, and it is the frame.
- **Zulfiia Dasaeva ran the partial scholarship work**: contacting recipients,
  finding out what would work for them, building custom coupons. The partials are
  not a discount code. They are one person negotiating with people individually.
  That is a better story than the total.
- **The notes' own line:** "An organization that gives away more access than it
  sells has a claim to legitimacy." Worth checking whether that is literally true
  before using it, but if the ratio holds it is the strongest sentence available.
