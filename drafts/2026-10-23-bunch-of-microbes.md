# The microbes line

It closed the meeting, over a full-bleed field image. Nine words, funny, and
scientifically literal, which is the rare combination.

From the notes: it "does the rare thing of being both a joke and the actual
scientific claim", and it works as a homepage closer, an email sign-off and a
social caption.

Full-bleed field photograph, dark scrim, the line set large in `--glow`. Same

## One thing to get right

**Do not use the ten-to-one ratio.** The old claim that human bodies carry ten
microbial cells for every human cell has been revised down substantially and is
now closer to parity. It is still repeated everywhere, including by people who
should know better, and using it would be exactly the kind of unattributed
statistic this account has been careful about.

The caption above says "in numbers comparable to your own cells", which is
defensible without a citation. **If a specific number is wanted on the card,
somebody has to source it properly**, and it is not needed for the joke to land.
