## Two things from the notes that improve the invitation

**Half off for Indian residents.** The workshop is $5,000 for existing students
including room, board and meals, and Indian residents pay half. That is a real
access decision and it belongs on the events card, because the audience this post
most wants to reach is the one that assumes it is priced for Americans.

**Evan's framing, which is better than anything I would write:** putting the
offering in front of people closest to the land rather than only Western, US and
Eurocentric audiences who can afford thousands of dollars.

**A price on a card needs Stephanie's sign-off.** She owns promotional content
and this crosses into it. The Google Ad Grant rejection makes a commercial-reading
card a live risk, so if in doubt the card says "reduced rate for Indian residents,
details on the school site" and the number stays off.
