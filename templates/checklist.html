Take action Before the first frost

The growing season is over. The soil season is not.

1
Compost now, not in spring It is an inoculant. The organisms get all autumn to spread.
2
Leave the residue It is food. Bacteria and fungi turn it into the glues that hold soil in crumbs.
3
Get something living in the ground A cover crop is for the roots. Living roots feed the organisms around them all winter.
4
Cover bare soil Under snow the biology slows. In an open winter it freezes hard and dries out.
