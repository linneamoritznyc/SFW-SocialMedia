Myth1 of 7

What you hear

Plant beans next to your tomatoes. They fix nitrogen and feed them.

So the beans go in between the tomatoes, the nitrogen never arrives, and the bag of fertiliser comes out anyway.

What is actually happening

The nitrogen goes into the bean. It reaches the bed when the bean dies and decomposes.

Instead

Chop the beans in at season's end. This year's beans feed next year's tomatoes.
