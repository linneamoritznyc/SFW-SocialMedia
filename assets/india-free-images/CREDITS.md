⚑ **The photographer names and addresses above are derived from the Unsplash
filenames, not read off the photo pages.** unsplash.com is blocked by this
environment's network policy, so the names could not be checked against the
site. The eleven character photo id in each address comes straight from the
filename and is reliable; the display names are a best reading of the
username slug and are worth one pass by eye before this table is quoted
anywhere public.

## How to fill a row

1. `Filename` exactly as uploaded, including the extension.
2. `Photographer` as Unsplash shows the name on the photo page.
3. `Unsplash URL` the photo page, not the direct image file.

That row is the record. Nothing is printed on the frames.
