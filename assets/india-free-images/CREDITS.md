⚑ **The photographer names and addresses above are read off the Unsplash
filenames, not the photo pages.** unsplash.com is blocked by this
environment's network policy. The eleven character photo id in each address
comes straight from the filename and is reliable; the display names are a
best reading of the username slug and are worth one pass by eye before this
table is quoted anywhere public.

## How to fill a row

1. `Filename` exactly as uploaded, including the extension.
2. `Photographer` as Unsplash shows the name on the photo page.
3. `Unsplash URL` the photo page, not the direct image file.
