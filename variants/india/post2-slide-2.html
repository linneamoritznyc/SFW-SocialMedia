Flooding and tilling do two things at once

Water fills the pore space. Oxygen goes with it. The root zone turns anaerobic for weeks at a time.
Tillage cuts the network. Fungal hyphae are threads. A pass of steel is a pass through all of them.
Bacteria tolerate both. Fungi tolerate neither.
