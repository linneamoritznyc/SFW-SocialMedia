It comes back in an order too

Which is why the first season of recovery rarely looks like much.

1Bacteria. Fastest to arrive, first to respond to food.
2Protozoa. They eat the bacteria, and that is where plant-available nitrogen starts.
3Beneficial nematodes. Grazing higher up the web.
4Fungi. Last, slowest, and the ones that rebuild structure.
