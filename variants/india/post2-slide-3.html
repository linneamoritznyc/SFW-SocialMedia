So the fungi go first

They need oxygen, and flooded soil has none.
They grow as a physical network, and tillage breaks it.
They rebuild slowly. Bacteria divide in hours; a hyphal network takes seasons.

Lose the fungi and you lose what they were doing.

The glues and threads that hold soil crumbs together. The slow release of nitrogen that favours your crop over fast weeds.
