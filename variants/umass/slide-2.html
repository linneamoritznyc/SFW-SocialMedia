The bigger picture September 2026

UMass Amherst announced its Future of Food Initiative this month.

$120 million, in four parts of $30 million each.

1A new Center for the Future of Food
2A 36,000 square foot research building, ground broken late 2027
3Five permanent endowments, including a chair in food science

Three tranches are committed: a family foundation, the Commonwealth of Massachusetts, and the university. The fourth is still to be raised.

Source: UMass Amherst news release, 14 September 2026.
