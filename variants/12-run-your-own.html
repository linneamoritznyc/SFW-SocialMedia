How to run a trial on your own ground

The design Sandra used in Van, Texas. Copy it.

1
Split one field, not three Same soil, same slope, same water. Differences between fields are not results.
2
Always keep a control One plot gets nothing but water. Without it you cannot tell biology from weather.
3
Measure before you start Organic matter at day one. A gain means nothing without a starting number.
4
Write down what goes wrong Hail, rodents, weeds. The failures are what make the numbers readable later.

Save this for spring.
