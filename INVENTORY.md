# INVENTORY

Every image, video, GIF, font, icon, logo variant and colour token kept in this
repository, with its path, its dimensions or duration, and what it depicts.

Written 17 September 2026, during the conversion of the Soil Food Web Foundation
website repository into a social media content workspace. Dimensions and
durations were read out of the file headers, not guessed from filenames. Where a
description says "verified by eye" the file was opened and looked at; everything
else is read from the alt text the old site carried for that exact file, from
`docs/`, or from the filename where the filename is plainly descriptive.

## How to read this file

- **⚑** means a human has to decide something about this file. Either nobody
  could say what it shows, or it carries branding, burned-in text or a licence
  question that makes it unsafe to post without checking. Nothing marked ⚑ was
  deleted.
- **·dup** means the file duplicates another file in this list at a different
  size or under a different name. Both were kept; the better one is named in the
  description.
- Sizes are on disk. Dimensions are pixels. Durations are seconds.

`/asset` searches this file. Descriptions are written so that searching for
"hands", "compost", "microscope", "nematode" or "aerial" finds the right thing.

- `mar-group-photo-2.jpg` and `workshop-group-around-compost-pile.jpg` are
  byte-identical. The second name is the accurate one: it is a workshop group
  around a compost pile, not a generic March group photo.

  and `(kopia)`, `MAR Group Photo 2.jpg`, `MAR25 Group Photo.jpg`,
  `Loida Teaching 3.JPG`, `Elaine behind Microscope.jpg`. Nothing was lost.
- Filenames that had spaces, capitals, commas or parentheses were normalised to
  lowercase and hyphens. The output of this repo is asset paths that get pasted
  into other tools, and a path with a space in it breaks on the way.
- **Dr. Elaine Ingham photographs carry Legacy Purple** and no other accent. See
  `sop/visual-rules.md` once it exists.

### The three colours that are restricted

Carried forward from the stylesheet comments, because they are the ones that get
broken by accident:

- **`--legacy` `#6B4C7A` Legacy Purple is for Dr. Elaine Ingham content only.** Not
  for general accents, not for anything else historical.
- **`--gold` `#C9A227` Harvest Gold is for the donate call only.** Not a highlight
  colour.
- **`--green-legacy` `#59A66C` is decorative only and never carries text.** It
  fails contrast. `--green` `#156826` is the one that does the work.

And one that is not a colour rule but behaves like one: **`--panel` `#F4F1EA` is a
shape, never a background.** The page is white.

### The brand board disagrees in two places

`assets/logo/sfw-amoeba-brand-board.png` names six swatches taken off a single
microscopy frame. Four match the stylesheet exactly: Living green `#A2AE77`,
Glow `#DBE6A7`, Membrane violet `#9E8FC2`, Deep `#3C3841`. Two are on the board
and **not in the stylesheet at all**: Slide `#B0B5AC` and Light `#D5DBC3`.

The board also names **Newsreader Light and Inter** as the typefaces. The site
shipped **Montserrat, Source Sans 3 and EB Garamond**, and those are the three in
`assets/font/`. ⚑ Somebody has to say which is current before a Canva template
gets built on the wrong one.
